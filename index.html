Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Ag-Panel and Standing Seam metal roofs?
Ag-Panel (Tuff-Rib) uses exposed screws driven through the 36-inch wide face of the metal panel into the purlins below. Standing Seam panels (12" to 18" wide) interlock over hidden clips with zero exposed screws, eliminating potential screw-washer leaks.
How many screws are needed per square of metal roofing?
For 36-inch wide corrugated panels, plan for approximately 75 to 80 screws per roofing square (100 sq ft). Screws are driven on both sides of every high rib along the eaves and ridge, and every other rib across interior purlins.
How far should metal roof panels overhang into the gutter?
Metal panels should overhang the fascia board or drip edge by 1.5 to 2.0 inches. Overhanging less than 1.5 inches causes rainwater to wick backwards onto the fascia; overhanging more than 2 inches risks water shooting over the gutter in downpours.
